Privacy notice & governance

Purpose. FollowUp helps workspace members manage business leads, contact details, notes, and follow-up tasks. Only enter information needed for a legitimate customer or prospect relationship.

Workspace access. Access is intended to be controlled by workspace membership, role, and lead assignment. Do not assume that this prototype has been independently security-audited. Workspace owners should remove access promptly when a teammate leaves and periodically review members and invitations.

Data minimisation. Avoid sensitive personal data, special-category data, payment-card details, identity documents, passwords, or confidential information in lead fields and notes. Notes can be more sensitive than ordinary contact fields; keep them factual and relevant.

GDPR information — not a compliance certification. Where the GDPR applies, the organisation deciding why and how lead data is used is generally the controller; FollowUp’s role depends on how it is operated and hosted. The controller should identify a lawful basis, provide transparent notices, honour applicable access/rectification/erasure/restriction/objection rights, maintain records where required, set retention periods, protect data, assess international transfers and processors, and handle personal-data breaches within applicable deadlines. This page is a starter template, not legal advice or proof of GDPR compliance.

Retention and deletion. Define a retention period for inactive leads, notes, exports, and activity history. Delete data when no longer needed, subject to legal obligations. Exported files are outside this app’s access controls; store them securely and delete copies when no longer needed. Activity history may contain historical snapshots until the database retention/redaction migration is applied.

Security and backups. Use unique passwords and MFA where available, restrict access to trusted people, keep browser/client keys public-only, never expose service-role keys, and test backups by restoring them. Database policies and RPC functions require periodic review and role-based tests. Do not treat the app as production-ready for sensitive customer data until those checks are complete.

Third-party services. Authentication and CRM storage use the configured Supabase project. Direct Google Drive upload and email delivery are not currently integrated. Before production, document processors, hosting locations, retention, contracts/data-processing terms, and international transfer safeguards as applicable.

Contact and updates. Replace this template with your organisation’s legal name, privacy contact, applicable jurisdiction, actual retention schedule, subprocessors, and a process for privacy requests before publishing it to customers. Update this notice whenever your practices change.

Owner governance checklist

  • Review workspace members and pending invitations monthly and after staff changes.
  • Use least-privilege roles; test that removed users lose access, including in existing sessions.
  • Set a retention schedule for leads, notes, activity logs, and exports.
  • Run and test encrypted backups and restoration before relying on the CRM.
  • Document who handles privacy requests, security incidents, and customer-data deletion.
  • Test workspace isolation, role escalation, exports, and activity-log access before launch.
